📖 POWER-UPS & MINIONS ALMANAC

Fire Flower

Acts like a Peashooter. Fires high-temperature fireballs at oncoming minions in its lane.

Cost: 100 Coins | Damage: 20 | Recharge: Fast

Superstar

Acts like a Sunflower. Periodically radiates gold and produces valuable coins to fund defenses.

Cost: 50 Coins | Production: 25 Coins | Recharge: Fast

Super Mushroom

Acts like a Wall-nut. Possesses massive durability (4000 HP) to withstand waves of minion bites.

Cost: 50 Coins | HP: 4000 | Recharge: Slow

Bob-omb

Acts like a Cherry Bomb. Sizzles for ~1 second, then unleashes a colossal 3x3 blast (1800 DMG)!

Cost: 150 Coins | Damage: 1800 (AoE) | Recharge: Very Slow

Bullet Blaster

Acts like a Repeater. Dual-cannon iron tower that fires 2 Bullet Bills at a time in rapid succession.

Cost: 200 Coins | Damage: 20 x 2 | Recharge: Fast

Ice Flower

Acts like a Snow-pea. Fires sub-zero frost balls that slow enemy movement and bites by 50%!

Cost: 175 Coins | Damage: 20 + 50% Slow | Recharge: Fast

Yoshi's Egg

Acts like a Potato Mine. Takes ~14s to hatch. When a minion steps on it, Baby Yoshi swallows them in one gulp and dashes off!

Cost: 25 Coins | Instant KO | Arm Time: 14s

Tanooki Leaf

Acts like an Umbrella Leaf. Protects power-ups in a 3x3 radius from Paragoomba snatch and drop attacks with a swift tail spin!

Cost: 100 Coins | 3x3 Sky Protection | Recharge: Fast

Lakitu's Cloud

Acts like a Lily Pad. Floating fluffy cloud planted on water tiles, allowing non-aquatic power-ups to be planted on top!

Cost: 25 Coins | Water Carrier | Recharge: Very Fast

Frog Suit

Acts like Tangled Kelp. Aquatic suit planted directly in water. Snaps its elastic tongue to drag an oncoming minion under!

Cost: 25 Coins | Water Only / Single-Use KO | Recharge: Slow

Goomba

Common minion of Bowser's army. Slow waddling footsteps and standard bite damage.

HP: 180 | Speed: Normal

Flag Goomba

Lazy minion that leads the charge on final waves of each level, waving Bowser's flag!

HP: 180 | Wave Leader | Only on Final Waves

Green Koopa Troopa

Quick footed foot-soldier. Moves faster than Goombas.

HP: 220 | Speed: Fast

Red Armored Koopa

Hardened red shell acts like armor, absorbing twice as much damage.

HP: 420 | Tough Armor

Hammer Bro

Elite soldier who periodically tosses heavy hammers at the front power-up in lane!

HP: 520 | Ranged Hammer Attack

Paratroopa (Winged)

Flies swiftly over the first power-up it encounters before dropping its wings.

HP: 240 | Vaults over 1st obstacle

Shy Guy (Heavy Armor)

Possesses massive health! Its hardened white mask absorbs 550 HP of damage before cracking and shattering, exposing a frantic unmasked runner!

HP: 200 + 550 Mask Armor (750 Total!) | High Durability

Boo

Acts like a Newspaper Zombie. Approaches shyly with hands covering its face. Once damaged enough, it reveals its terrifying face and rushes at high speed!

HP: 350 | Enraged Speed Boost | Ghostly Minion

Boom Boom

Acts like a Dancer Zombie. Tough brawler who winds up and summons 4 Spinies around himself in cross formation (above, below, left, right)!

HP: 600 | Summons 4 Spinies | Heavy Brawler

Spiny

Spiked shell minion spawned by Boom Boom. Protected by razor-sharp spikes.

HP: 200 | Spiked Shell | Summoned Minion

Paragoomba (Bungee Drop)

Acts like a Bungee Zombie. Drops from above to steal a power-up or deploy surprise minions! Countered by Tanooki Leaf.

Aerial Threat | Plant Thief / Minion Drop | Deflected by Tanooki Leaf
